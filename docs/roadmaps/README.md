# Roadmaps

Practical takeaway: only roadmaps with `status: active` and a pending/open
slice are executable. Completed or closed roadmaps are receipt indexes.

Use roadmaps together with their current campaign card and the decision log.
Do not restart work from a roadmap whose slices are all `completed`; use its
receipts as historical evidence and draft a new campaign card for follow-up
work.

The `source_agenda` and `doctrine` fields may point at closed PRDs or agenda
cards. Those links are provenance, not permission to revive the closed plan.
