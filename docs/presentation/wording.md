I would not say “heartbroken” directly. Not because it is false, but because it asks them to hold your feeling before they have re-engaged with the work. Better:

“I was genuinely disappointed by where we ended, because I believed in the direction of the product and still do.”

That communicates sincerity without emotional pressure.

The core move is this:

“I can see now that I was trying to solve too many problems at once: prove the product, move at prototype speed, and also stay tightly aligned with an existing codebase and another developer’s conventions. The newer version separates those concerns and shows the product path more clearly.”

That is very good. It owns your part without quietly prosecuting them.
